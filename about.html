Why We Cap Ratios

We turn people away on purpose.

Every case manager at Stackly caps how many clients they'll take on. It means we say no more often than we'd like — and it means the clients we do take on get seen, every single week.

No case manager carries more clients than they can actually watch closely A waitlist exists so quality doesn't slide when demand spikes Every check-in gets a real reply — never a queue of unread messages

What We Believe

Three rules we don't bend on.

Systems beat motivation

A good case routine keeps moving even on the weeks you don't feel like it. We build the process so willpower isn't the thing holding your growth together.

Case management is weekly, not quarterly

A dashboard that flags your numbers isn't a case manager. Every client gets a real human checking their case plan and their week, every single week.

Honest numbers over hype

We measure, we re-measure, and we tell you the real number — even when it's not the one you wanted to hear that month.
